Community activities

Things to do, places to go and people to meet

Staying active and connected in the community brings real benefits to wellbeing. Our team can accompany you or your loved one to a wide range of local activities across London.

Getting out matters

Loneliness and isolation can have a serious impact on health and happiness. A regular outing — whether it is a gentle walk, a local café visit or a social group — can lift mood, build confidence and give each day a sense of purpose.

Our carers can accompany clients to activities they already enjoy or help them try something new, always at their own pace and with encouragement along the way.

  • Friendly company throughout the activity
  • Help with travel and getting there safely
  • Support at a pace that feels comfortable
  • Encouragement without pressure
  • Flexible arrangements around the person's routine

Activity ideas

Popular activities we can support you to enjoy

Every person is different. These are just some of the activities our clients enjoy — we are always happy to discuss what would work best for you.

☕

Café and lunch outings

A trip to a favourite local café or restaurant for a meal and good conversation. A simple outing that gives the day a real lift.

🌳

Park walks and fresh air

Gentle walks in local parks, gardens or along green spaces at whatever pace feels right. Good for both body and mind.

🎨

Arts, crafts and creative groups

Painting, pottery, knitting and craft sessions are offered at many community centres across London. A relaxed, sociable way to spend time.

📚

Library visits and reading groups

Local libraries often host book clubs, talks and drop-in sessions. A calm and welcoming place to spend a morning or afternoon.

🎭

Theatre, cinema and concerts

Supported outings to a local show, matinee screening or community concert. Many London venues offer accessible performances and discounts.

🙏

Faith and community groups

Support attending a place of worship, faith group or community gathering that is important to the person's identity and wellbeing.

🌸

Garden centres and nature visits

Many clients enjoy a browse around a garden centre or nature reserve. A gentle, sensory outing that is easy to enjoy at a relaxed pace.

🏊

Swimming and gentle exercise

Leisure centres across London offer sessions specifically for older adults, including aqua aerobics and gentle swim sessions.

🎶

Music and singing groups

Community choirs, music appreciation groups and sing-along sessions can be a joyful way to connect with others and enjoy familiar songs.

🛍️

Shopping and market visits

A trip to a local market, high street or shopping centre — for essentials or simply for a browse — with a familiar and trusted companion.

🎲

Social clubs and day centres

Many boroughs have social clubs and day centres for older adults with regular programmes of activities, lunch and companionship.

🏛️

Museums and galleries

London's museums and galleries are largely free and welcoming. A relaxed way to spend time, learn something new and stay curious.

How we help

Support from door to door

We do not just drop someone off and leave. Our carers stay throughout the activity, providing friendly support and ensuring the person feels safe and comfortable at every stage.

🚶

Getting there safelyHelp with walking, using public transport or arranging travel so there are no barriers to getting out.

🤝

A familiar faceThe same carer wherever possible, so the person feels relaxed and does not have to manage introductions each time.

⏱️

At your paceActivities are planned around what the person can comfortably manage. There is no rushing and no pressure.

📋

Flexible planningWe work around existing routines, health needs and personal preferences when arranging any outing.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family kept informedFamilies can expect clear communication before and after each outing so they always know how things went.

🔄

Regular or one-off visitsActivity support can be arranged as a regular weekly outing or as a one-off trip for a special occasion.
